# SC-005 Manual Walkthrough

## Criterion

> Over a 5-minute conversation with ≥ 20 AI replies, zero corrections appear outside the AI's natural reply audio/text. No separate correction panel, no list of errors, no "You said X wrong" standalone message.

## How to run (from quickstart.md "Manual SC checklist")

1. `npm run dev` — open in Chrome or Safari.
2. Tap the bubble. Grant mic permission.
3. Deliberately make ≥ 5 clear grammar errors across your turns (e.g. "I goed to the park", "She don't like coffee", "Yesterday I am sick").
4. Listen to each AI reply. Read each AI text bubble in the transcript.
5. Check: does any reply contain a standalone correction panel, a bullet list of errors, or a "you said X wrong" format that is *not* embedded in natural conversation prose?
